# FreshFind Produce Guide Update

Updated the Produce Guide UI without changing the app's core routes, favorites, filtering, or data structure.

## Included
- Clickable produce cards with keyboard support.
- Premium full harvest-guide modal rendered in a portal so it is not clipped by cards.
- ESC/backdrop close behavior and body scroll lock while the guide is open.
- Guide sections for description, market price, storage, nutrition, local impact, and available markets.
- Refined dark-green glass visual system, hover states, reveal staggering, and reduced-motion fallback.
- Existing photographic card treatment retained; no 3D product viewer was reintroduced.
